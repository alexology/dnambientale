Research themes

We turn environmental samples into ecological knowledge.

Molecular methods and fieldwork to monitor biodiversity quickly, sensitively, and with minimal disturbance to ecosystems.

The method

Detecting species without ever seeing them

Environmental DNA flips the traditional approach on its head: genetic material suspended within environmental matrices can be extracted to reveal which species are present.

The result is faster, gentler on ecosystems, and sensitive enough to catch elusive species that are hard to monitor, such as alien species in the early stages of invasion or species of conservation concern.

«A single sample of water, soil or air can hold the signature of an entire community.»
