Biographies

Who we are

Dr. Alex Laini

Research lead

PhD in Ecology, currently a researcher in zoology at the University of Turin. Specialising in the conservation of aquatic environments, since 2018 developing and applying DNA metabarcoding-based methods for monitoring aquatic invertebrates. Responsible for experimental design, data analysis, bioinformatics and building collaborations with water basin management authorities.

Dr. Samuele Voyron

Lab manager

Environmental genomicist specialising in invasive species. Completed a PhD working on qPCR assays for early warning, and currently developing frameworks for detecting invasive crayfish and mussels before they become established.

Laura Gruppuso

POSTDOC

PhD student in Ecology, working on airborne eDNA as a tool for monitoring terrestrial biodiversity. This doctoral project extends the group's sampling methods beyond the aquatic environment.

Gianluca Natta

POSTDOC

Develops and maintains the group's analysis pipelines, from raw read processing to taxonomic assignment. Also works on benchmarking metabarcoding methods and data quality.
